# My work — Exercise 2

1. Pp × Pp.

   |   | P  | p  |
   |---|----|----|
   | P | PP | Pp |
   | p | Pp | pp |

   The genotypes come out PP : Pp : pp = 1 : 2 : 1.

   Only PP has two purple alleles, so only PP plants are purple.
   A Pp plant has a white allele in it, so it comes out white like pp.

   Phenotype ratio: purple : white = **1 : 3**.

2. Pp × PP.

   Every offspring gets a P from the PP parent, and half get the p from the Pp parent.
   Half are PP and half are Pp, so half the offspring have white flowers.
