# Resources — Political theory from first principles

## Verified

- Anthony Weston, *A Rulebook for Arguments*, 5th ed. (Hackett, 2017) — premises, conclusions and how an argument is put together.
- Jonathan Wolff, *An Introduction to Political Philosophy*, 3rd ed. (Oxford University Press, 2016) — the standard introduction.
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, "Positive and Negative Liberty" (Ian Carter) — https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/liberty-positive-negative/
