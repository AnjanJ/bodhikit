# Practice task — Mayonnaise (saved before the rep)

**The rep (20 minutes):** one yolk, a teaspoon of mustard, 200 ml neutral oil, by whisk. The first 50 ml drop by drop.

**Watch for:** when it turns from runny to thick; what the whisking arm feels; any sign of oil pooling.

**Debrief questions (asked before anything is recorded):**
1. What did you do, step by step, with quantities and times?
2. What went wrong or surprised you?
3. What would you change next time, and why?
