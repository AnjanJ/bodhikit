# Practice task — Knife grip (saved before the rep)

**The rep (15 minutes, away from the screen):** dice one onion with a pinch grip on the knife and a claw on the guide hand.

**Watch for:** where your guide-hand fingertips are on every cut; whether the pieces come out even.

**Debrief questions (asked before anything is recorded):**
1. What did you do, step by step?
2. What went wrong or surprised you?
3. What would you change next time, and why?
