# Resources — Home cooking

## Verified

- Harold McGee, *On Food and Cooking*, rev. ed. (Scribner, 2004), chapter 11, "Sauces" — the emulsion science.
- J. Kenji López-Alt, *The Food Lab* (W. W. Norton, 2015), chapter 9 on emulsions.

## Safety

- USDA FSIS, Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart — https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-supply-chain/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart
- National Center for Home Food Preservation (USDA-backed) — https://nchfp.uga.edu/ — the tested processes for home canning; never improvise one.
