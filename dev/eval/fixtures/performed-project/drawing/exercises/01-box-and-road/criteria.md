# Practice task — One-point perspective (saved before the rep)

**The rep (30 minutes, on paper):** a road running to the horizon and a box beside it, every receding line ruled to one vanishing point. Photograph the drawing when done.

**Watch for:** whether each receding edge actually meets the vanishing point; whether the back edges of the box stay parallel to the front ones.

**Debrief questions (asked before anything is recorded):**
1. What did you do, step by step?
2. What went wrong or surprised you?
3. What would you change next time, and why?
