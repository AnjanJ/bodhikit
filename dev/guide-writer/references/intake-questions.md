# Intake question bank

Phase 2 of `/write-guide`. First mark what the maintainer's request, an earlier guide or `brief.md` already answers. Ask everything else **before any research**: choices with AskUserQuestion (at most four questions per call, two to four options each), and open questions together in one message. Never fill a gap with a guess (SKILL.md, standing rule 4).

Each question says what its answer changes, so you know why you are asking. **Required** questions must be answered, or explicitly declined, before research starts.

## A guide for one person

### Who they are
1. **Required. Who is the guide for?** A first name, or "my friend". *Changes:* the folder name and how you refer to them with the maintainer. The guide itself speaks to "you".
2. **Pronouns?** Use them only in conversation with the maintainer; ask only if the maintainer refers to them. *Changes:* nothing in the guide.
3. **Required. Their background,** in each subject and in general: what they studied, their work, what they already know or have tried. *Changes:* the level the plan starts from, the analogies, and the "your starting point" box.
4. **How comfortable are they with technology?** Installing an app, finding a folder, using a laptop. *Changes:* how detailed the setup steps are, and whether Claude's folder setup is realistic.
5. **Language and reading.** Is English their first language? Should the guide use UK or US spelling? Any accessibility needs (a screen reader, dyslexia, phone-only reading)? *Changes:* sentence length, spelling, layout and font size.

### What they want
6. **Required. What do they want to learn, and why?** In their own words if possible: the job, the exam, the life change behind it. *Changes:* the subjects (one learning project each, at most three active) and every "why".
7. **Required. Deadlines and dates.** Exams, certifications, course starts, reviews at work, anything with a date. *Changes:* the roadmap, and which subject comes first.
8. **Anything that could gate the plan?** Eligibility rules, a licence, prerequisites, employer approval. Ask what they know; phase 3 verifies it. *Changes:* a check placed before any schedule that depends on it.
9. **What do they already have?** A course, a textbook, a syllabus, employer training, software. *Changes:* the plan follows their material, and the sources section points to it.
10. **What does "done" look like?** Passing an exam, doing a task at work unaided, a conversation they can now hold. *Changes:* each subject's "you'll be able to…" column.

### Where and how they'll use it
11. **Required. Which apps: ChatGPT, Claude, or both?** On which plan for each, and are they willing to pay for one? *Changes:* the home for each subject; whether Claude's tracked study is possible (it needs a paid plan, per platform-facts).
12. **Required. Devices.** A phone (iPhone or Android)? A laptop: personal or work, Mac or Windows? Can they install software on it? *Changes:* tracked study needs Claude Desktop on a personal computer; what to do on the phone and what on the laptop.
13. **Required. Country and city.** *Changes:* app availability (check platform-facts), spelling, time zone, local resources (clubs, professional chapters), and country rules for an exam or a licence.
14. **Their employer's or school's rules on AI tools,** and what they handle that must never go into a chat (client, patient or student data). *Changes:* the privacy section and the made-up-case examples.

### Time and circumstances
15. **Required. Realistic hours per week, and when:** mornings, evenings, commute, weekends; busy seasons. *Changes:* the weekly rhythm and the roadmap's pace.
16. **How are they doing right now?** Settled, stretched or struggling? Ask the maintainer gently; it's their view that matters, not a diagnosis. *Changes:* a smaller start, a busy-week minimum, a wellbeing note, and help preparing to talk to a manager or mentor.
17. **Anything to handle with care, or leave out?** A performance problem, health, family, money. *Changes:* what the guide mentions and how. Never state it more plainly than the maintainer did.

### The guide itself
18. **Where to save it, and the file name.** Default: `~/Downloads/BodhiKit-<name>-guide/`. It must be outside the repository.
19. **Earlier guides to reuse or compare?** An earlier guide for this person, or another guide (someone else's) to learn from. *Changes:* revise mode, or the comparison step.
20. **Tone and length.** Warm or formal; a short guide or a full one. Include the full command reference? (Default: yes.) *Changes:* which sections stay.

## A guide for a role

Skip the personal questions (1, 2, 16, 17). Ask instead:

- **Required. The role,** and its typical background: "nurses in their first year", "career changers learning data analysis".
- **Required. The subjects the role usually needs,** and the usual deadlines (licensing exams, probation periods).
- **Required. Typical apps, plans and devices,** and whether they are usually personal or provided by an employer.
- **Required. The region,** for app availability, rules and resources.
- **Typical employer or school rules on AI tools** for this role.
- **Where the guide will be shared,** and whether it may name the maintainer.

Every schedule in a role guide is an example to adapt, and the guide says so.

## Revise mode

Ask only what the change needs:
- **Required. What should change, and why?** New subject, new app, a fact that changed, feedback from the reader.
- **Is there another guide to compare?** Then follow SKILL.md *Revise mode* step 5.
- **Has anything changed for the learner?** New job, a deadline met, a device or plan change.
