Part A: Getting started

1. What BodhiKit is, and why it asks so many questions

BodhiKit is a tutor that you add to [ChatGPT or Claude]. It works for any subject: [their subjects]. Ordinary [ChatGPT or Claude] hands you a finished answer. BodhiKit teaches the way a good tutor does. It finds out what you already know, makes a plan, explains one idea at a time, gives you something to do yourself, and asks you to explain it back.

It will ask you questions when you just want the answer. That is on purpose. In a study of high-school maths students, those who used a ChatGPT-style assistant did much better while practising, then scored worse in the exam without it than students who never had AI. A version that guided them instead of answering removed most of that harm (Bastani et al., PNAS, 2025). In a study of programmers learning something new, those who handed the work to the AI remembered the least, and those who asked questions to understand kept most of it (Shen & Tamkin, 2026). Quizzing yourself helps in every subject studied (Yang et al., Psychological Bulletin, 2021).

So the questions are the learning. But you set the pace: when you want an explanation now, or you're short on time, say so (section [N]).

5. Set up ChatGPT (web, desktop app, phone)

Install (once)

  1. Open ChatGPT on the web (chatgpt.com), in the desktop app or in the phone app.
  2. Choose Plugins in the sidebar.
  3. Search for BodhiKit. Check that the publisher is Abhaya Code Labs.
  4. Open it and choose Install plugin.
  5. Start a new chat. A plugin is available in chats started after you install it.

Start BodhiKit in a chat

Type @, choose BodhiKit, and in the same message say which subject and what you want.

@BodhiKit Continue my [subject]. Here is my note from last time: …

Does it stay on?

QuestionAnswer
Install again for each chat or subject?No. Once.
Type @BodhiKit in every new chat?Yes, make it a habit. ChatGPT sometimes picks the plugin by itself, but not always.
Before every message in one chat?No. Once at the start; again if you switch activity, or if replies start handing you finished answers.
Does it remember me next chat?No. Your learning log carries your plan and review dates. Paste your latest note at the start of each chat.

[N]. Reviews and your learning record

You forget a new idea fast unless you recall it again after a gap, and each successful recall makes the next gap longer. BodhiKit uses gaps of 1 → 3 → 7 → 14 → 30 days.

  • Recalled it without help? Move it to the next gap.
  • Wrong, or needed a hint? Back to 1 day.
  • Not due yet? Leave it. Reviewing early helps less than waiting for the gap.
